Reliable but Invalid评分表的幻觉

Open source

Evaluation design · corpus engineering · statistical analysis — 2026评估设计 · 语料工程 · 统计分析 — 2026

A self-improvement loop had a model write novel openings, score them against a genre rubric, and rewrite its own instructions from the low-scoring dimensions. The scores plateaued. This project is the work of finding out why — and the answer turned out to be about the measuring instrument, not the writer. 一个自我改进循环：模型写小说开头、用评分表给自己打分、再据低分项改写写作指令。分数卡住不动。这个项目就是追查原因的过程——而答案不在写手身上，在那把尺子上。

  • Built an 840-item benchmark across 37 genre × channel cells from a 1,110-novel corpus, with contamination exclusions computed per cell from what the pipeline actually read — an audit of which surfaced a leak the hard-coded rule had missed.用 1,110 部小说的语料建成 840 项基准，覆盖 37 个品类×频道格子；污染排除按各格管线实际读过的内容逐格计算——自查这个接触面时发现了硬编码规则漏掉的一处泄漏。
  • Showed a human-vetted rubric explained under 2% of reader-behaviour variance despite high test–retest reliability (ICC = .87). Reliability and inter-judge agreement — the two standard warrants for LLM-as-judge evaluation — cannot detect this failure.证明经人工审核的评分表只能解释读者行为方差的不到 2%，而其重测信度高达 ICC = .87。信度与评审间一致性——业界给 LLM 评审背书的两个标准手段——都查不出这种失效。
  • Found that judge choice reverses the conclusion: four models across three vendors disagreed by 3.6 points on identical texts, including two capability tiers from a single vendor. Sampling across vendors does not mitigate it.发现结论取决于谁来评：四个模型、三家厂商，对同一批文本的判断跨度 3.6 分，其中两个还是同一厂商的不同档位。多找几家厂商投票并不能缓解。

Webnovel Auto

In development开发中

Genre research · corpus design · editorial workflow — 2026类型研究 · 语料设计 · 编辑流程 — 2026

An end-to-end AI writing and publishing workflow for Chinese web fiction. Each platform and each genre — xuanhuan, romance, urban — gets its own corpus, so the model learns how that particular readership expects a chapter to open, turn and end. Generated chapters then publish and update on a schedule. 面向中文网文的端到端 AI 写作与发布流程。按站点和题材分别建库——玄幻、言情、都市各成一套语料，让模型学会该类读者期待的开篇、转折与收尾。生成章节按计划自动发布与更新。

  • The interesting part is not generation but taste: deciding what a genre's conventions actually are, and where a model breaks them badly enough that a reader drops the book.有趣的不是生成，而是判断力：确定某一类型的成规究竟是什么，以及模型在哪里破坏它到读者弃书的程度。
  • Comparative evaluation of generation workflows across multiple model providers.跨多家模型供应商对生成流程做对照评测。
