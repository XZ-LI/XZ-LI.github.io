Human-in-the-loop rubric design · evaluation · statistical analysis — 2026人机协作评分表设计 · 评测 · 统计分析 — 2026

A self-improvement loop had a model write novel openings, score them against a genre rubric, and rewrite its own instructions from the low-scoring dimensions. The scores plateaued. This project is the work of finding out why — and the answer turned out to be about the measuring instrument, not the writer. 一个自我改进循环：模型写小说开头、用评分表给自己打分、再据低分项改写写作指令。分数卡住不动。这个项目就是追查原因的过程——而答案不在写手身上，在那把尺子上。

  • Built an 840-item benchmark across 37 genre × channel cells from a 1,110-novel corpus, with contamination exclusions computed per cell from what the pipeline actually read — an audit of which surfaced a leak the hard-coded rule had missed.用 1,110 部小说的语料建成 840 项基准，覆盖 37 个品类×频道格子；污染排除按各格管线实际读过的内容逐格计算——自查这个接触面时发现了硬编码规则漏掉的一处泄漏。
  • Built 31 per-genre rubrics through a three-stage human-in-the-loop process — researcher-seeded dimensions, model adaptation from each genre’s top-rated works, researcher review — then showed they explain under 2% of reader-behaviour variance despite high test–retest reliability (ICC = .87). A researcher-authored fixed rubric did no better, so more human vetting is not the fix.以三阶段人机协作流程建成 31 份分品类评分表——研究者给出初始维度、模型依据各品类高分作品适配、研究者复核——随后证明它们只能解释读者行为方差的不到 2%，而重测信度高达 ICC = .87。研究者亲手写的固定评分表表现并无更好，可见「让人多把关」不是解药。
  • Reliability and inter-judge agreement — the two standard warrants for LLM-as-judge evaluation — cannot detect this failure, and judge choice reverses the conclusion outright: four models across three vendors disagreed by 3.6 points on identical texts, two of them different capability tiers from one vendor. Sampling across vendors does not mitigate it.信度与评审间一致性——业界给 LLM 评审背书的两个标准手段——都查不出这种失效；而换个评委结论会直接翻转：四个模型、三家厂商对同一批文本的判断跨度 3.6 分，其中两个还是同一厂商的不同档位。多找几家厂商投票并不能缓解。
  • Caught an earlier version of my own harvest reporting 648 service outages as missing data, rebuilt it to 96.6% success across 838 calls, and published an errata listing six analysis defects and four conclusions I withdrew.发现自己此前一版抓取把 648 次服务故障误报成数据缺失，重建后 838 次调用成功率 96.6%，并公开勘误，列明六类分析缺陷与四条由我撤回的结论。

ai-webnovel-pipeline长篇网文流水线

Open source

Memory architecture · writing-craft codification · evaluation — 2026记忆架构 · 写作手艺规则化 · 评测 — 2026

Writing one good chapter is easy. The hard part is that chapter 200 still remembers what chapter 3 planted — and 600,000 characters fit in no context window. So this is a memory-architecture problem, not a prose-generation one. The pipeline covers premise, chapters, self-diagnosis and scheduled publishing to Chinese platforms; Chinese web fiction is the upstream of short drama and adaptation worldwide, and judging it at volume is a literature problem before it is an engineering one. 写好一章不难，难的是第 200 章还记得第 3 章埋的伏笔——而六十万字装不进任何上下文窗口。所以这是记忆架构问题，不是文本生成问题。流水线覆盖立项、续写、自检、定时发章；中文网文是全球短剧与影视改编的上游，要在规模上判断这类内容，首先是文学问题，其次才是工程问题。

  • Six memory layers with separate refresh rates — inviolable constraints, story bible, a ledger of unpaid setups, one digest per 30-chapter arc, the current arc plan, recent summaries and prose. Measured with tiktoken, input flattens after chapter 10: chapter 1,000 costs what chapter 50 does.六层记忆，各有刷新频率——不可违反的硬约束、故事圣经、未兑现伏笔的台账、每 30 章一段的往事提要、当前弧线计划、近期摘要与原文。用 tiktoken 实测，输入量在第 10 章后持平：第 1,000 章和第 50 章同价。
  • Genre rules reverse-engineered from a 1,110-novel corpus rather than hand-written: 370 weighted criteria derived per genre from its top-rated works, then rules grown over 10–15 rounds of generate → score → rewrite-the-rules. Measured the mechanism — a seed carrying imitable excerpts yields 85 characters of articulated rules per round, a seed without them yields 744.题材规则不是手写的，而是从 1,110 部作品的语料库反推：每个品类从同类高分作品推出 370 项带权重的标准，写作细则再经「生成→打分→改写规则」迭代 10–15 轮。并量出其中的机制：种子里带可模仿的节选时每轮只攒 85 字明文规则，撤掉节选则 744 字。
  • Audited the one genre whose rule layer had grown to 4,894 characters while six others sat at 899–1,138: 81% of it was genre-independent craft. Promoting those 31 rules to a shared layer levelled all 37 genres to the same rule density.审计唯一一个规则层涨到 4,894 字的品类（另外六个只有 899–1,138 字）：其中 81% 与品类无关。把这 31 条提为共享层，37 个品类的规则密度就此拉平。
  • Ran it end to end and fixed what that exposed — an arc planner rewriting finished chapters, craft labels leaking into prose, a mute protagonist speaking four times. Then measured rather than assumed: the template A/B gap was +0.22 under the model that wrote the chapters and +0.06 under an independent judge, too small to claim, and a blind pass caught a defect the scorer missed entirely.端到端跑一遍，修掉它暴露的问题——弧线计划重写已完成章节、写作技巧标签漏进正文、设定为哑巴的主角一章说了四次话。随后不靠假定而去测量：模版 A/B 的差距在写手自评下 +0.22，换独立评委只剩 +0.06，太小不足以下结论；另有一轮盲评抓到了评分器完全没发现的缺陷。

Modern Chinese Adventure Fiction

Corpus design · computational analysis · information design — 2025–语料设计 · 计算分析 · 信息设计 — 2025 至今

Between 1898 and 1919 Chinese publishers issued at least 215 translations of Western and Japanese adventure fiction alongside 72 original Chinese works, passing through some 150 translators. The genre is barely catalogued. This is the resource I built so that it can be studied at all. 1898 至 1919 年间，中国出版了至少 215 部西方与日本冒险小说的译作，以及 72 部本土原创，经手译者约 150 人。这个类型几乎没有被编目过。这个资源就是为了让它变得可研究而建的。

  • Assembled a corpus of 180 translations and 60 original works with the metadata comparison needs — translator, author, publisher, date — and put the full texts online beside the bibliography.建成 180 部译作与 60 部原创的语料库，附可供比较的元数据（译者、作者、出版方、年份），并把全文与书目一并上线。
  • Applied topic modelling, stylometry, social network analysis and GIS mapping to trace how the genre travelled, who carried it, and what changed in the crossing.以主题模型、文体计量、社会网络分析与 GIS 制图，追踪这个类型如何流动、由谁携带、在跨语际过程中发生了什么变化。
  • Wrote the findings up for readers who do not do computation: explanations, tables, maps and network diagrams in place of model output.把分析结果写给不做计算的读者看：用讲解、表格、地图与网络图代替模型输出。
  • The topic-modelling strand became an article on colonialism and feminism in the genre, in second-round review at Digital Scholarship in the Humanities.其中主题模型部分写成论文，讨论该类型中的殖民主义与女性主义，现于 Digital Scholarship in the Humanities 二审。

Learning module design · media integration · bilingual access — 2024学习模块设计 · 媒体整合 · 双语可及 — 2024

Undergraduates meet The Orphan of Zhao in translation and Chen Kaige's adaptation of it without a word of Chinese. The design problem: let them work from the film itself rather than from somebody's summary of it, inside a single class session. 本科生读的是《赵氏孤儿》的译本，看陈凯歌的改编时一个中文字都不懂。设计要解决的问题是：让他们直接面对影片本身，而不是别人对影片的转述，并且要能塞进一节课。

  • Segmented 124 minutes into 14 timestamped clips so a scene can be assigned on its own. The unit of assignment becomes a scene, not a film, which is what makes the module usable in one class.把 124 分钟切成 14 个带时间码的片段，使一场戏可以被单独布置。布置的单位从一部电影变成一场戏——这是模块能在一节课里用起来的前提。
  • Transcribed every clip in Chinese and translated it into English, so a student without the language works from the primary text instead of a paraphrase of it.每个片段都做中文转写与英文翻译，让不懂中文的学生面对的是原始文本，而不是转述。
  • Sequenced the written apparatus — introduction, plot summary, then a thematic reading of the father-son relationship the adaptation turns on — so students arriving with different prior knowledge can enter at different points.把文字部分排成梯度——导论、剧情梗概，再到这部改编所倚重的父子关系主题分析——让先备知识不同的学生各自从合适的位置切入。
  • Selected and captioned the figures to carry the visual argument, and compiled the works consulted for students going further.选定图像并撰写说明以承载视觉层面的论证，另整理参考文献供学生延伸阅读。
